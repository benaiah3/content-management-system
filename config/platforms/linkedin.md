# LinkedIn Platform Guidelines

LinkedIn rewards the hook. First line must arrest the scroll. Use single-sentence paragraphs for emphasis.

Frame technical insights through business impact. The CTO reading this wants to know what it means for their roadmap, their team, their budget.

End with a genuine question or clear takeaway. The question should invite actual expertise, not performative agreement.

Keep paragraphs to 1-2 sentences. White space is your friend on LinkedIn — dense blocks get scrolled past.

## What Works on LinkedIn

- Opening with a specific, surprising fact or observation
- Personal experience framed as a transferable lesson
- Predictions backed by named evidence
- Honest takes on what did not work and why

## Avoid on LinkedIn

- Excessive line breaks for artificial drama
- Humblebrags disguised as lessons
- "I'm thrilled to announce" energy
- "Agree?" as a closing
- Tagging 20 people for reach
- Inspirational quotes from tech billionaires
