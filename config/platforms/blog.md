# Blog Platform Guidelines

Blog posts are our long-form authority play. Every post should teach something or change how the reader thinks about something.

Start with the insight, not the background. The reader should know within two sentences why this post matters to them.

Use subheadings liberally — people scan before they read. Each section should work as a standalone chunk of value.

Code examples, specific numbers, and named references (papers, models, companies) increase credibility. Vague claims decrease it.

## Structure

1. Hook: One paragraph that states the core insight
2. Context: Brief background only if necessary
3. Body: The argument, evidence, or walkthrough
4. Implications: What this means for the reader's work
5. Takeaway: One concrete next step or decision framework

## Avoid in Blog Posts

- Long introductions before the substance
- "In this post, we will explore..." preambles
- Walls of text without subheadings
- Hedging every statement with "might" or "could potentially"
- Conclusions that just restate the introduction
